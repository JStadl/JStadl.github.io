There are some absolutely amazing courses available for free online, but nobody makes you do the work, the scheduling and bookkeeping is left to you, and the solutions are always one tempting click away.

This is my attempt at fixing those niggles. It keeps a public page of [my progress](https://jstadl.github.io/self-study-tracker/), does the planning for me, and keeps the solutions locked until I've had a proper go.

If you'd like one of your own, there's a [template](https://github.com/JStadl/template-self-study-tracker) you can copy.
